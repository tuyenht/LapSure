Non-negotiable:
- Evidence before verdict.
- Presence is not functionality.
- Factory mismatch is not hardware-health failure.
- Missing/malformed/timed-out/untrusted/permission-denied/unsupported evidence never becomes PASS.
- UNKNOWN, NOT TESTED, UNSUPPORTED and INCOMPLETE remain explicit.
- Required coverage incomplete => no clean BUY/PASS.
- Do not invent hardware measurements or confidence.
- Preserve native Windows/WinPE graceful degradation and external-engine trust boundary.
- Default user-facing language is Vietnamese.
