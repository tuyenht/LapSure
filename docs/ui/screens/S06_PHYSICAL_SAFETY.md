# S06 — Ngoại hình & An toàn

`components: [C01,C02,C03,C04,C08,C10,C11]`  
`visual: ../references/approved/S06_PHYSICAL_SAFETY.jpg`

## User outcome
Record physical/safety findings that electronic inventory cannot infer.

## Objects
Cracks/dents/warping; hinge; screws/tamper; liquid/corrosion; battery swelling; charger/cable safety; burning smell/electrical instability; photo/operator evidence.

## Data
Operator-confirmed physical evidence and safety findings.

## Invariant
Verified safety-critical defects may drive REJECT and cannot be softened by price/cosmetics. Missing/skipped mandatory safety evidence remains incomplete.

## Acceptance
Clear instructions, severity choices, photo/evidence traceability where supported, destructive/safety choices confirmed.
